I am an industrial Ph.D. student at TRATON Group and the Division of Decision and Control Systems, School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science, at KTH Royal Institute of Technology. I am advised by Karl H. Johansson.

My research focuses on learning and intelligent behavior in autonomous systems, with an emphasis on generalization, adaptation, and acquiring new behaviors. My current work spans reinforcement learning, adaptive dynamics modeling, and motion planning, with robotics and autonomous driving as real-world applications.

Research interests

  • Reinforcement learning
  • Generalization & adaptation
  • Robot intelligence

My broader goal is to build intelligent agents that keep learning and turn that knowledge into capable behavior in the physical world.
